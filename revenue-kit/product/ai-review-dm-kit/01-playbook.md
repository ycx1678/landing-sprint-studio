# AI Review & DM Reply Playbook

## Core Rule

Reply like a human who is calm, specific, and accountable.

A good reply has four parts:

1. Acknowledge the customer.
2. Mention the specific issue or request.
3. Offer the next step.
4. Keep the door open.

## Tone Modes

### Friendly

Warm, casual, and short. Best for cafes, studios, salons, creators, and repeat customers.

### Premium

Polished, restrained, and confident. Best for clinics, high-ticket services, hotels, boutiques, and professional services.

### Concise

Direct and practical. Best for DMs, delivery updates, booking confirmations, and busy inboxes.

### Apologetic

Accountable without overpromising. Best for delays, complaints, mistakes, and service recovery.

## Escalate Instead Of Replying From A Template

Escalate when the message involves:

- injury, illness, or safety concerns,
- threats, harassment, discrimination, or violence,
- legal claims or chargebacks,
- private personal data,
- minors,
- medical, financial, or legal advice,
- a customer asking for a guarantee you cannot verify.

## Review Response Formula

Positive review:

> Thank them, mention one detail, invite them back.

Negative review:

> Acknowledge, avoid arguing, offer a direct support channel, and keep private details private.

Neutral review:

> Thank them, name the improvement area, invite more feedback.
