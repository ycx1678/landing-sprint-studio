AI Review & DM Reply Kit

A practical library of customer reply templates, AI prompts, and reusable snippets for reviews, DMs, complaints, bookings, refunds, and follow-ups.

Start Here

  1. Open 02-template-library.md.
  2. Pick the closest template.
  3. Replace bracketed fields like [Name], [contact], and [link].
  4. Use 03-prompt-recipes.md to adjust tone with your AI assistant.
  5. Review the final reply before sending it to a real customer.

Included Files

Playbook

Tone rules, reply formula, escalation guidance, and safe AI editing instructions.

Templates

88 starter replies for reviews, DMs, bookings, complaints, refunds, follow-ups, and Korean examples.

Snippets

A CSV library for quick import into note apps, help desks, or internal team docs.

Use Safely

Do not use these templates to make medical, legal, financial, tax, employment, or guaranteed outcome claims. Escalate sensitive situations to a qualified professional.
