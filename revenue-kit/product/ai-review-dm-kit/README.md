# AI Review & DM Reply Kit

For small businesses that need faster, calmer customer communication.

## Included

- `index.html`: open this first for a simple visual guide.
- `01-playbook.md`: usage guide, tone rules, and escalation guidance.
- `02-template-library.md`: ready-to-use customer replies.
- `03-prompt-recipes.md`: AI prompts for custom replies.
- `04-snippets.csv`: importable snippet library.
- `license.md`: simple personal/business use license.

## How To Use

1. Pick the closest template.
2. Replace bracketed fields.
3. Add real context.
4. Ask your AI assistant to adjust the tone.
5. Review before sending.

Important: Do not use these templates to make medical, legal, financial, employment, or safety claims. Escalate sensitive situations to a qualified professional.
