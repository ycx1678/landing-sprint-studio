# Sources Checked

Checked on 2026-05-25.

- Payhip pricing: free plan has a 5% transaction fee; Stripe/PayPal fees still apply; immediate deposits are described on the pricing page. Source: https://payhip.com/pricing
- Gumroad fees: Gumroad help center describes a 30% fee for marketplace discovery sales and separate fee details for standard sales. Source: https://gumroad.com/help/article/66-gumroads-fees.html
- Upwork freelancer fee: Upwork says freelancer service fees range from 0% to 15% per contract, shown before proposal/offer, and locked once a contract starts. Source: https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211062538-Learn-about-the-Freelancer-Service-Fee
- Upwork freelancer workflow: Upwork supports Talent Marketplace proposals and Project Catalog offers. Source: https://www.upwork.com/i/how-it-works/freelancer/
- Fiverr seller earnings: Fiverr help says sellers earn 80% of the purchase amount, including extras and tips. Source: https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/9234443621137-Your-earnings-page
