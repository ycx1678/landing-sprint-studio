# Revenue Kit

This folder contains two lawful, launch-ready revenue paths:

1. **Service offer:** 24-hour landing page sprint for founders, local shops, coaches, creators, and agencies.
2. **Digital product:** AI Review & DM Reply Kit for small businesses.

The service route is the fastest path to cash because it can be sold before fulfillment. The digital product route is slower to validate but can be listed once and reused.

## Best First Move

Post the service offer first, then use the digital product as either:

- a low-priced product on Payhip/Gumroad,
- a free lead magnet for the service,
- or a bonus included with the service package.

## Suggested Prices

- Digital product: **$19** launch price, then **$29** after first 10 sales.
- Landing page sprint: **$249** starter, **$499** standard, **$799** priority.
- Hourly fallback for marketplaces: **$35-$55/hour**.

## What Needs Your Manual Approval

I prepared the assets, copy, templates, and static sales page. You still need to do these account-bound actions yourself:

- create or log into Payhip/Gumroad/Upwork/Fiverr/Kmong,
- connect a payout method,
- upload the product ZIP,
- publish listings,
- send outreach from your own account only after reviewing the message,
- accept and sign any client contracts.
